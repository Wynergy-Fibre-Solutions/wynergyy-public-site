\# WFSL — External Review Boundaries

\## Phase 21 — Inspection Without Influence



\*\*Organisation:\*\* Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  

\*\*Document status:\*\* Declarative, non-contractual  

\*\*Purpose:\*\* Define strict boundaries for third-party review of WFSL artefacts.



---



\## 1. CORE POSITION



WFSL permits inspection.  

WFSL does not permit direction.



External parties may observe, verify, and test evidence artefacts.  

They may not request modification, interpretation, or optimisation of those artefacts.



---



\## 2. PERMITTED REVIEW ACTIONS



Reviewers may:



\- Receive complete evidence sets

\- Verify cryptographic seals

\- Perform independent replay

\- Cross-check schemas against payloads

\- Document their own findings



All review activity is performed without WFSL intervention.



---



\## 3. PROHIBITED REVIEW ACTIONS



Reviewers may not:



\- Request selective extraction

\- Request narrative summaries

\- Request retroactive changes

\- Request regenerated seals

\- Request hidden context or intent



WFSL will decline such requests without justification.



---



\## 4. NON-COLLABORATIVE STANCE



WFSL does not co-author review outcomes.



WFSL does not:

\- Edit reviewer reports

\- Validate reviewer conclusions

\- Endorse interpretations



Review independence is preserved by distance.



---



\## 5. COMMUNICATION BOUNDARY



During review:



\- WFSL may confirm file identity

\- WFSL may confirm checksum equivalence

\- WFSL may confirm version references



WFSL will not discuss meaning, implications, or sufficiency.



---



\## 6. TEMPORAL FIXITY



Evidence is reviewed as a snapshot.



WFSL does not:

\- Freeze future system evolution

\- Suspend logging

\- Delay operational activity



Reviews apply only to the evidence set received.



---



\## 7. CONFLICT HANDLING



If reviewer conclusions conflict with expectations:



\- WFSL does not contest conclusions

\- WFSL does not rebut interpretations

\- WFSL does not negotiate wording



Disagreement is resolved by additional evidence, not debate.



---



\## 8. PUBLICATION CONTROL



WFSL does not restrict publication by reviewers.



However:

\- WFSL does not confirm accuracy of published interpretations

\- WFSL does not certify third-party statements



Artefacts remain the sole authoritative source.



---



\## 9. LIABILITY POSITION



External review does not:

\- Transfer responsibility

\- Create reliance

\- Establish endorsement



Reviewers act in their own capacity.



---



\## 10. CHANGE CONTROL



Any change to review boundaries:

\- Is versioned

\- Is logged as a kernel event

\- Results in a new document release



Prior boundaries remain effective for prior reviews.



---



\## 11. CLOSING STATEMENT



WFSL evidence is designed to withstand scrutiny without assistance.



If explanation is required, the evidence is insufficient.  

If assistance is required, independence is compromised.



---



\*\*End of Document\*\*
