\# WFSL — Evidence Handover Protocol

\## Phase 20 — Controlled Disclosure and Third-Party Transfer



\*\*Organisation:\*\* Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  

\*\*Document status:\*\* Descriptive, non-contractual  

\*\*Purpose:\*\* Define how WFSL evidence artefacts are transferred without interpretation or alteration.



---



\## 1. PRINCIPLE



WFSL evidence is transferred as \*\*artefacts\*\*, not explanations.



The recipient is expected to:

\- Verify integrity independently

\- Perform their own analysis

\- Draw their own conclusions



WFSL does not curate outcomes.



---



\## 2. WHAT MAY BE TRANSFERRED



A handover may include:



1\. Event logs  

2\. Seal artefacts  

3\. Replay outputs  

4\. Schemas  

5\. Version identifiers  

6\. Inspection documentation



Each artefact is optional and explicitly listed.



---



\## 3. WHAT IS NEVER TRANSFERRED



WFSL does not provide:

\- Interpretive summaries

\- Legal opinions

\- Compliance assertions

\- Selective extracts presented as conclusions



Evidence is transferred whole or not at all.



---



\## 4. ARTEFACT PREPARATION



Before handover, WFSL ensures:



\- Files are complete and readable

\- Seals correspond to included logs

\- Replay artefacts reference the same inputs

\- No artefact is modified post-sealing



No reformatting is performed for presentation.



---



\## 5. TRANSFER METHOD



Artefacts may be transferred via:



\- Encrypted archive

\- Physical media

\- Secure file transfer



The method is recorded as metadata, not embedded into the artefacts.



---



\## 6. CHAIN OF CUSTODY DECLARATION



At handover time, WFSL records:



\- Date and time of transfer (UTC)

\- Artefact list with checksums

\- Transfer method

\- Declared recipient identity



This declaration is itself an event.



---



\## 7. POST-HANDOVER POSITION



After transfer:



\- WFSL does not alter the artefacts

\- WFSL does not retract evidence

\- WFSL does not amend logs



Any subsequent change requires a new, separate evidence set.



---



\## 8. RECIPIENT RESPONSIBILITIES



Recipients are expected to:



\- Validate hashes

\- Perform replay independently

\- Preserve artefact integrity



WFSL is not responsible for post-transfer handling.



---



\## 9. DISPUTE HANDLING



If discrepancies are alleged:



\- WFSL may reissue the same artefacts

\- WFSL will not reinterpret prior evidence

\- WFSL will not regenerate seals retroactively



The original artefacts remain authoritative.



---



\## 10. SCOPE LIMITATION



This protocol:

\- Does not imply acceptance by any authority

\- Does not guarantee regulatory sufficiency

\- Does not establish contractual obligation



It governs \*\*technical evidence transfer only\*\*.



---



\## 11. CHANGE CONTROL



Any change to this protocol:

\- Is versioned

\- Is logged as a kernel event

\- Results in a new document seal



Previous versions remain accessible.



---



\## 12. CLOSING STATEMENT



WFSL evidence handover is designed to be boring, mechanical, and verifiable.



Trust is optional.  

Verification is mandatory.



---



\*\*End of Document\*\*
