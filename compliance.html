International Compliance Statement

Effective date: v1.0

Purpose

ACE is a governed explainer system designed to provide informational explanations about Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd, its operating principles, and its governance posture. ACE does not provide advice, make decisions, or act autonomously.

Data Protection and Privacy (ACE v1)

  • ACE v1 does not collect, store, or process personal data.
  • No cookies, tracking, analytics, or profiling are used.
  • No automated decision-making or behavioural analysis occurs.

As a result, ACE v1 operates outside the scope of personal data processing under EU GDPR, UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and comparable privacy laws internationally.

Licensed and Private Access (ACE v2)

Where licensed, private access may be provided under explicit terms. Processing is limited to contractual necessity and explicit consent where required. Data minimisation and purpose limitation are enforced. No profiling or automated decisions are performed. Rights of access and erasure are supported.

AI and Automation

  • ACE is an informational system only.
  • ACE does not provide legal, financial, or operational advice.
  • Final decisions remain the responsibility of human directors and officers.

Under the EU AI Act, ACE is assessed as minimal-risk or outside scope when used as a governed explainer.

Jurisdiction

  • Governing law: England and Wales
  • Jurisdiction: UK courts

International Use

ACE is not targeted to specific jurisdictions. Users access the system voluntarily. Public operation does not rely on localised marketing, tracking, or behavioural profiling.

Change Control

Public disclosures and system behaviour are versioned and governed. Changes are deliberate, documented, and auditable.

Plain-English Summary

  • ACE v1 is public and does not collect personal data.
  • ACE v2 is private, licensed, and governed by explicit terms.
  • We do not run tracking, profiling, or automated decision-making.
  • We keep WFSL operations separate from other brands and initiatives.
