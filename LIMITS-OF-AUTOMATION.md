\# WFSL — Limits of Automation

\## Phase 22 — Explicit Human Boundary Conditions



\*\*Organisation:\*\* Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  

\*\*Document status:\*\* Declarative, non-contractual  

\*\*Purpose:\*\* Define explicit limits on what WFSL systems automate, infer, or decide.



---



\## 1. PRINCIPLE STATEMENT



WFSL systems automate process, not judgement.



Automation is used to:

\- Record

\- Order

\- Seal

\- Replay



Automation is not used to:

\- Decide legality

\- Infer intent

\- Replace human responsibility



---



\## 2. WHAT IS AUTOMATED



WFSL automation is limited to deterministic functions:



\- Event capture at runtime

\- Schema validation

\- Ordering and sequencing

\- Cryptographic sealing

\- Replay verification



All automated actions are observable and attributable.



---



\## 3. WHAT IS NOT AUTOMATED



WFSL systems do not automate:



\- Legal interpretation

\- Compliance determination

\- Risk acceptance

\- Enforcement decisions

\- Human accountability



No system output constitutes advice, instruction, or authority.



---



\## 4. NO INFERENTIAL ENGINES



WFSL does not operate systems that:



\- Infer intent

\- Predict behaviour

\- Score individuals

\- Classify risk without disclosure



All outputs correspond directly to explicit inputs.



---



\## 5. HUMAN DECISION REQUIREMENT



Where judgement is required:



\- WFSL systems halt

\- Evidence is presented

\- A human decision is required



The system records the decision but does not generate it.



---



\## 6. AUDIT CLARITY



Automation boundaries are designed so that an auditor can determine:



\- What the system did

\- What the system did not do

\- Where human responsibility began



Ambiguity is treated as a design failure.



---



\## 7. FAILURE MODE TRANSPARENCY



When automation fails:



\- Failure is logged

\- State is preserved

\- No fallback inference occurs



Silence is not permitted as a failure response.



---



\## 8. AI POSITIONING



Where AI-assisted tooling exists:



\- It is non-authoritative

\- It is advisory only

\- Its outputs are not binding



AI outputs are never sealed as evidence.



---



\## 9. USER EXPECTATION CONTROL



WFSL does not represent automation as:



\- Decision-making

\- Compliance certification

\- Risk mitigation



Automation supports visibility, not outcomes.



---



\## 10. CHANGE CONTROL



Any expansion of automation scope:



\- Is explicitly documented

\- Is reviewed as a design change

\- Results in new artefacts and seals



Historical limits remain applicable to historical evidence.



---



\## 11. CLOSING STATEMENT



WFSL automation exists to make truth inspectable, not to replace judgement.



Where a human must decide, the system steps back.



---



\*\*End of Document\*\*
