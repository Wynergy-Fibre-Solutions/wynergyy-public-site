\# WFSL Evidence Catalogue

\## Phase 12 — Evidence Index, Classification \& Disclosure Control



\*\*Organisation:\*\* Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  

\*\*Document Status:\*\* Declarative, non-contractual  

\*\*Purpose:\*\* To enumerate technical evidence artefacts produced by WFSL systems and define their disclosure posture.



---



\## 1. FUNCTION OF THIS CATALOGUE



This document provides a structured index of evidence artefacts generated by WFSL systems.



It exists to:

\- Prevent ambiguity about what evidence exists

\- Avoid ad-hoc disclosure

\- Support proportionate, scoped inspection



This is an index, not a disclosure.



---



\## 2. EVIDENCE GENERATION MODEL



WFSL evidence is:

\- Generated at runtime

\- Append-only

\- Deterministic

\- Cryptographically verifiable



Evidence is not reconstructed retrospectively.



---



\## 3. EVIDENCE CLASSES



\### 3.1 Kernel Event Logs



\*\*Description:\*\*  

Ordered records of system-level events.



\*\*Characteristics:\*\*  

\- Append-only  

\- Time-stamped (UTC)  

\- Actor-attributed  



\*\*Typical Files:\*\*  

\- `\*.event-log.json`



\*\*Disclosure Status:\*\*  

Available on request, scope-limited.



---



\### 3.2 Runtime Ownership Artefacts



\*\*Description:\*\*  

Proof of which live process owned execution authority at a given time.



\*\*Characteristics:\*\*  

\- Process-bound  

\- Time-bound  

\- Explicit lifecycle  



\*\*Typical Files:\*\*  

\- `\*.lock.json`



\*\*Disclosure Status:\*\*  

Incident-bound only.



---



\### 3.3 Cryptographic Seal Artefacts



\*\*Description:\*\*  

Hashes proving integrity of evidence at a point in time.



\*\*Characteristics:\*\*  

\- SHA-256 based  

\- Immutable once issued  



\*\*Typical Files:\*\*  

\- `\*.seal.json`



\*\*Disclosure Status:\*\*  

Paired with disclosed logs only.



---



\### 3.4 Replay Validation Outputs



\*\*Description:\*\*  

Independent verification results confirming integrity and order.



\*\*Characteristics:\*\*  

\- Deterministic  

\- Non-destructive  



\*\*Typical Files:\*\*  

\- `\*.replay.json`



\*\*Disclosure Status:\*\*  

Optional, supporting material.



---



\### 3.5 Event Schemas



\*\*Description:\*\*  

Formal definitions of allowable event structures.



\*\*Characteristics:\*\*  

\- Versioned  

\- Human-readable  



\*\*Typical Files:\*\*  

\- `\*.schema.json`



\*\*Disclosure Status:\*\*  

Freely shareable.



---



\## 4. DISCLOSURE PRINCIPLES



WFSL evidence disclosure follows these rules:



1\. \*\*Minimum Necessary\*\*  

&nbsp;  Only evidence relevant to the inquiry is disclosed.



2\. \*\*Time-Bounded\*\*  

&nbsp;  Evidence is scoped to defined periods.



3\. \*\*Paired Integrity\*\*  

&nbsp;  Logs are never disclosed without their seals.



4\. \*\*No Narrative Substitution\*\*  

&nbsp;  Evidence is not summarised in place of disclosure.



---



\## 5. WHAT IS NOT EVIDENCE



The following are explicitly excluded from evidence classification:



\- Debug output

\- Console logs

\- Developer commentary

\- Screenshots

\- Reconstructed timelines



Only runtime artefacts qualify.



---



\## 6. THIRD-PARTY VERIFICATION



External parties may:

\- Verify hashes

\- Replay events

\- Inspect schemas



Without WFSL tooling or mediation.



---



\## 7. RETENTION POSTURE



Evidence retention is:

\- Purpose-bound

\- Declared

\- Non-speculative



No indefinite or silent retention occurs.



---



\## 8. CHANGE CONTROL



Changes to:

\- Evidence structure

\- Classification

\- Disclosure posture



Are logged as kernel events and versioned.



---



\## 9. LIMITATIONS



This catalogue:

\- Is not a disclosure commitment

\- Does not guarantee availability

\- Does not imply legal obligation



It defines capability only.
