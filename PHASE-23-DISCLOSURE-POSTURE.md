# WFSL — Disclosure Posture

## Phase 23 — Controlled Transparency & External Disclosure

**Organisation:** Wynergy Fibre Solutions Ltd (WFSL)  
**Document status:** Declarative, non-contractual  
**Purpose:** Define how WFSL discloses system information, evidence, and artefacts to third parties without over-exposure or misrepresentation.

---

## 1. POSITIONING

WFSL operates a controlled disclosure model.

Disclosure is:
- Intentional
- Contextual
- Evidence-based

Disclosure is not:
- Continuous
- Unbounded
- Interpretive

---

## 2. DISCLOSURE OBJECTIVES

WFSL discloses information to enable:
- Inspection
- Audit
- Due diligence
- Verification

WFSL does not disclose information to:
- Persuade
- Certify
- Assert authority

---

## 3. DISCLOSURE TIERS

### 3.1 Public Disclosure

May include:
- High-level system descriptions
- Architectural principles
- Non-operational documentation

Excludes:
- Live runtime data
- Internal identifiers
- Process ownership artefacts

---

### 3.2 Request-Based Disclosure

May include:
- Event logs (time-bounded)
- Sealing artefacts
- Replay outputs

Conditions:
- Purpose is declared
- Scope is defined
- Artefacts are immutable

---

### 3.3 Restricted Disclosure

Includes:
- Incident-specific evidence
- Chain-of-custody artefacts
- Process attribution records

Access is:
- Explicit
- Logged
- Reviewable

---

## 4. NO IMPLIED AUTHORITY

Disclosure does not imply:
- Compliance certification
- Regulatory approval
- Legal endorsement
- Fitness for purpose

Interpretation remains the responsibility of the recipient.

---

## 5. FORMAT STANDARDS

All disclosed artefacts are:
- Machine-readable
- Human-reviewable
- Non-proprietary

Primary formats:
- JSON
- Markdown

No proprietary viewer is required.

---

## 6. REDACTION PRINCIPLES

Redaction is applied only to:
- Protect unrelated parties
- Prevent unnecessary exposure
- Maintain scope discipline

Redaction is itself disclosed and documented.

---

## 7. TEMPORAL BOUNDARIES

WFSL does not disclose:
- Forward-looking projections
- Hypothetical future states
- Simulated outcomes

Only recorded, sealed artefacts are disclosed.

---

## 8. DISCLOSURE LOGGING

Every disclosure event:
- Is recorded
- Is timestamped
- References disclosed artefacts

Disclosure activity is auditable.

---

## 9. THIRD-PARTY HANDLING

Once disclosed:
- WFSL does not control interpretation
- WFSL does not curate conclusions
- WFSL does not amend historical artefacts

Evidence stands as issued.

---

## 10. CHANGE CONTROL

Changes to disclosure posture:
- Are versioned
- Are documented
- Do not apply retroactively

---

## 11. CLOSING STATEMENT

WFSL discloses enough to verify truth, and no more.

Transparency is deliberate, not performative.

---

**End of Document**
